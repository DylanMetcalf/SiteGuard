        06 · See the improved outcome

        The same file, before and after

        Same documents, same people, same site. What changes is where the information lives and how easy it is to see its state.

        Before

        • Information spread across inboxes, chats and folders.
        • Status difficult to confirm.
        • Outstanding items discovered late.
        • Repeated manual follow-ups.
        • Uncertainty about what happens next.

        With COMVERA

        • Each document filed against the requirement it answers.
        • Clear statuses: missing, waiting for review, approved, expiring.
        • Expiries and gaps flagged by the compliance agent before the gate.
        • Requests and notes recorded on the item, not in someone's inbox.
        • Every item shows whose move it is next.
